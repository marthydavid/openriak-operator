The operator watches RiakCluster, RiakBucket and RiakUser resources in all namespaces.
Default operand image: {{ .Values.riak.image }}

RiakUsers authenticate with mTLS client certificates, issued by cert-manager or
supplied from an external CA. cert-manager must be installed for the node
certificate of TLS-enabled clusters:
  https://cert-manager.io/docs/installation/

Quick start:
  kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l control-plane=controller-manager
  kubectl apply -f https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marthydavid/openriak-operator/main/examples/0-local-dev-cluster.yaml
